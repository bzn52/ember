OUR STORY

ROOTED
IN
NEPAL.

EMBER began with a question we kept asking ourselves: could the food we grew up eating look and feel completely different, without losing what made it good?

From the spice markets of Asan to farms in the hills surrounding Kathmandu, we work with local producers to bring Nepal's ingredients into a modern kitchen.
